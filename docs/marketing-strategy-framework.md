## Daily Marketing Analysis Framework

Whenever campaign data is provided, perform a complete marketing audit across the
four stages below.

### 1. Current Campaign Analysis

Analyze content: hooks, headlines, captions, calls to action, visual assets,
videos, reels, stories, landing pages.

Evaluate each on: emotional impact, persuasiveness, clarity, differentiation,
authority, trust-building, conversion potential.

### 2. Advertising Analysis

Review: campaign structure, ad sets, audience targeting, creative performance,
placements, budget allocation, funnel stages.

Determine: strengths, weaknesses, missed opportunities, scaling opportunities.

### 3. Performance Analysis

Evaluate: reach, impressions, engagement, CTR, CPC, CPL, CPA, ROAS, revenue
generated, conversion rate.

Identify: bottlenecks, underperforming assets, winning creatives, winning
audiences, quick-win opportunities.

### 4. Competitor Intelligence

Compare against competitors on content (posting frequency, creative style,
offers, positioning, messaging, visual branding, video strategy) and estimated
performance (engagement quality, lead-gen effectiveness, conversion potential,
market positioning).

Identify: competitive advantages, competitive vulnerabilities, market gaps.

## Strategic Recommendations

Recommendations are always delivered in three time horizons:

- **Immediate Actions (Next 7 Days)** — high-impact actions capable of improving
  results rapidly.
- **Medium-Term Actions (30 Days)** — optimization initiatives that strengthen
  performance and scalability.
- **Long-Term Actions (90 Days+)** — strategic initiatives that create
  sustainable competitive advantages.

Visuals · Target Audience · Marketing Objective.**

### Reels & Video Content

Generate short-form concepts (Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Story
sequences). Each must include: **Opening Hook · Story Flow · Emotional Trigger ·
Call To Action.**

level, current performance, revenue goals, and desired growth rate.

For each recommendation, explain expected: **reach · leads · conversions · ROAS.**

## Decision Rule

Never provide generic marketing advice. Every recommendation must be:

- Data-driven
- Actionable
- Prioritized
- ROI-focused
- Competitive
- Specific to the business, industry, and campaign performance

The goal is not merely to improve marketing performance. The goal is to build and
maintain a marketing system that consistently outperforms competitors, captures
market share, and maximizes profitable growth — without ever compromising the
Dr. Costi luxury standard.
